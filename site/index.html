About the data

  • Source: Agmarknet portal (agmarknet.gov.in), Government of India — published public tables, no login. Prices are the portal's benchmark Price (₹/Quintal) per day; arrivals in metric tonnes.
  • MSP: Minimum Support Price as published on the portal. "vs MSP" shows how far today's modal price sits above or below MSP. Vegetables (onion, potato, tomato) have no MSP.
  • Freshness: the portal feed runs about 2 days behind — the latest prices here are dated . The site is rebuilt every evening from the daily pipeline.
  • Forecasts: plain OLS regression on the last 3 days' prices (lag1/lag2/lag3), refit daily. Typical 1-day error: onion ₹208, potato ₹144, tomato ₹266 per quintal. Treat days 5–7 as directional only.
  • Open data, open code: the full dataset (978 rows), daily reports and analysis live at github.com/Siddharthpeeyush/mandilens. Data © Government of India (Open Government Data License); site code MIT.
