# Data

## Source

Live data comes straight from the Government of India's published Agmarknet
portal tables — **no API key needed**:

- **Portal:** [agmarknet.gov.in](https://agmarknet.gov.in/home) → "Market Wise
  Price & Arrival" (public, no login) — 24 commodities across 5 groups
  (cereals, fibre crops, oil seeds, pulses, vegetables), with 3 days of prices,
  arrival volumes, and MSP per commodity
- **Method:** once a day, the published table is read (via browser) and
  normalized by `src/parse_browser_extract.py` (exact format:
  `src/BROWSER_FETCH_BRIEF.md`)
- Each row carries the portal's benchmark **Price (Rs./Quintal)** per day, mapped
  to `modal_price` (`min_price`/`max_price` stay empty — the portal publishes a
  single benchmark, not a range), plus `arrival_tonnes` and `msp_rs`

`src/fetch_prices.py` (data.gov.in API) remains as an *optional* fallback: set
`MANDILENS_DATA_GOV_KEY` (free key from [data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in) →
My Account → API Key Management) and the pipeline prefers it. The daily job
runs keyless by default.

## Sample data (status)

`data/raw/` holds **30 sample files** (29 Sep – 29 Oct 2026, generated by
`tools/make_sample.py` — seeded RNG, reproducible, clearly fake; 13 Oct
missing) and **15 portal files** (13 National, 30 Oct – 11 Nov 2026, plus
2 per-mandi extracts, 10–11 Nov). Every daily report and chart states its
source, so sample vs portal data is never ambiguous. The per-mandi sample
feed stopped at 29 Oct; new days come from the portal (National benchmark
only — 24 commodities, 3 days of prices per pull — plus per-mandi
Onion/Potato/Tomato rows from 10 Nov), and the sample files remain as the
project's early history.

## Known gap — per-mandi days 30 Oct – 05 Nov 2026 (Day 32 backfill check)

The per-mandi feed has an 11-day hole, 30 Oct – 09 Nov 2026, between the
sample feed's end (29 Oct) and the first per-mandi portal pull (10 Nov).
The portal publishes only a rolling 3-day window, so the check
(`src/backfill_check.py`, `data/processed/backfill_check_2026-11-11.csv`)
classifies all 88 market-days in the hole:

- **Recovered: 10 market-days, 18 real rows** — 06–09 Nov for Azadpur,
  Bowenpally and Sealdah Koley Market (the only 3 of 8 mandis whose
  portal tables ever carry vegetables). Sealdah reports Tomato only;
  Bowenpally has no Onion row.
- **Permanently lost: 21 market-days, 63 commodity rows** — 30 Oct –
  05 Nov × 3 reporting markets. Those dates slid out of the portal's
  3-day window before any per-mandi pull ran, and no other source
  publishes them. They are gone, not delayed — forecasts for those
  dates train on the sample feed, and the gap is visible in every
  per-mandi series.
- **Still in the window: 0 market-days — resolved 13 Nov 2026** — Azadpur
  08 Nov has no rows in any of the 10/11/12 Nov pulls, and Bowenpally
  09 Nov was EMPTY on the 11, 12 and 13 Nov pulls: both are confirmed
  never reported, not delayed. The 11-day hole is now fully classified.
- **Never obtainable: 55 market-days, 165 rows** — the other 5 mandis
  (Vashi/APMC Mumbai, Koyambedu, Yeshwanthpur/Bengaluru APMC, Naroda,
  Muhana) yield no vegetable rows from this portal at all (not listed,
  no data, or no vegetables in the table), so their 30 Oct – 09 Nov
  rows never existed to recover.

Net: of the 264 commodity rows the 8-mandi × 11-day hole could in
principle hold, 63 are permanently lost, 165 were never obtainable,
18 are recovered, and 6 never reported (2 market-days × 3 commodities).

## Known behaviour — first-seen dedup vs portal revisions (Day 34)

The portal revises its National figures as late market reports arrive
(price median |Δ| 0.21%, arrival median 3.02% between pulls; worst
case Jowar 09/11 arrival 69.4 → 34.9 t). But `clean.py`'s
`drop_duplicates` keeps the FIRST pull's value for each (market,
commodity, date), so **146 of 354 clean rows on overlapping keys (41%,
13 Nov 2026) differ from the latest published figure** — the clean
dataset records values as first published, not as finally published.
Per-mandi rows are unaffected (18/18 exact across pulls). Switching
to keep-latest would rewrite 146 historical values; that is a data
decision for a follow-up, not a bug fix. Audit:
`src/arrival_audit.py`, `data/processed/arrival_audit_2026-11-13.csv`,
`reports/day34_arrival_data_quality.md`.

## Layout

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data/raw/` | One CSV per day, as fetched (portal) or as generated by `make_sample.py` (sample) |
| `data/processed/mandi_prices_clean.csv` | Tidy, deduplicated, analysis-ready dataset (1,104 rows, 29 Sep – 09 Nov 2026) |
| `data/processed/*.csv` | Per-day analysis tables (rolling features, forecasts, accuracy, data quality) |

## Columns

`state, district, market, commodity, variety, grade, arrival_date (DD/MM/YYYY),
min_price, max_price, modal_price, arrival_tonnes, msp_rs` — all prices in
₹/quintal; arrivals in metric tonnes. Portal rows carry a single benchmark
price per day (`min_price`/`max_price` empty); sample rows carry a full
min/max/modal range.
